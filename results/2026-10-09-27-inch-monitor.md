- **Date:** 2026-10-09

## What the user wants

27-inch monitor for a MacBook Air. Budget PKR 40k–60k max. 1440p fine, 4K welcome. General /
media use, wants good colours. USB-C to USB-C connection appreciated (about 30–65W charging is
enough for an Air). Refresh rate not a priority.

## Recommendation

No **new** 27" monitor with USB-C and 1440p/4K is under PKR 60k at the stores checked. The
cheapest new ones with USB-C were ViewSonic VP2756A-2K (107,999) and HP M27fd (1080p, 71,999)
at Shophive.

- **Top pick (used):** Dell U2720Q, PKR 34,999 on OLX Karachi. 4K, factory-calibrated wide
  colour range, one cable with 90W charging.
- **1440p pick (used):** Dell U2723DE, PKR 29,500 on OLX Lahore. Best contrast and colour of the lot.
- **Used with buyer protection:** the Lenovo (44k) or Dell P2725DE (53k) on Daraz.
- **New, no USB-C:** MSI PRO MP273U 4K (60,999), plus a USB-C to DisplayPort cable.
